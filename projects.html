How it works

Six straightforward steps, from your very first message to commercial furniture positioned in your room.

01

Tell us about your project

Message us on WhatsApp with your drawings, your furniture schedule, or just an idea. No forms, no account, no waiting.

02

We reply

Someone from the team answers, usually the same day. They'll ask the questions that save trouble later on.

03

Confirm the order

Pieces, quantities, materials, price and lead time — all agreed in writing before anything moves.

04

Samples and specifications ★

We produce a sample, and you choose the exact fabrics, timbers, finishes and dimensions you want.

05

Approve the sample ★

Nothing goes into production until you've seen it and said yes.

Delivered to your project

We coordinate shipping, documentation and delivery, so you can get on with opening.
